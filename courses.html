How we teach

Realistic,
not textbook

Our team has spent more than a decade teaching in Egypt, the Philippines, Cambodia, Vietnam and Indonesia, and we bring the same standard here: clear briefings, sites chosen by the tide rather than the timetable, and dive plans built around the people actually on the boat.

  • Maximum four students per instructor
  • Teaching in Spanish, English, French, Dutch, Hebrew and Russian
  • New 2024 equipment, checked daily
  • Digital SSI certification issued on the spot
  • Courses start at Koh Ma — calm and current-free
  • Maximum 20 people on board, always
