Try Dive

The first time you breathe underwater is a strange and very good feeling, and it should not be rushed. This is one full day with a maximum of two students per instructor — genuine attention, not a queue. We start in the calm, current-free water at Koh Ma, and if you are comfortable, Sail Rock is on the table too.

Sail Rock · from the surface

Everyone · 1 day

Snorkelling
at Sail Rock

Sail Rock rises from 40 metres to above the surface, which means the good stuff starts in the first few metres. Come for the boat ride and stay at the top of the water — this is how families keep everyone on the same trip, and how nervous first-timers work out whether they want to go further down.

Coming as a group,
or with children?

Tell us the ages, the experience levels and your dates, and we will build a day that works for all of you on one boat — divers below, snorkellers above. Hotel pick-up in Chaloklum and around is usually no problem.

Ask us on WhatsApp
What is included in every price?

Your SSI instructor or guide, full equipment (regulators, BCD and wetsuit, XS to XXL, all new as of 2024), snacks and water on board, and the underwater video of your dive sent to you afterwards.

What time do we leave and get back?

We depart at 10:00 from Chaloklum, which puts us on the sites while other boats are already heading home. You will be back at the pier in the afternoon. Check in with us at the shop before departure so there is time for paperwork and sizing.

Can I do a Fun Dive if I haven't dived in years?

Yes. Tell us how long it has been when you message and we will plan the first dive as a refresher — shallow, calm, and unhurried — before anything more ambitious.
