An SSI centre
in Chaloklum

We opened in January 2024 with a clear idea: safe diving, small groups, and a relaxed atmosphere. Our SSI instructors have more than ten years leading dives in Egypt, the Philippines, Indonesia and Thailand — and that standard now lives here, aboard our own boat.

Why it matters

Why choose a centre
with its own boat?

Max 20 on board

Small groups

Most boats depart with 30+ divers. We limit ourselves to 20. Fewer people means a more attentive instructor, equipment assembled at a relaxed pace, and more space on deck between dives.

10:00 from Chaloklum

A later departure

Our 10:00 departure takes you to Sail Rock and Koh Ma when other boats are already heading back. You get dive sites with fewer people on them and better visibility.

Conditions permitting

Multi-site days

For certified divers we visit several sites around Koh Phangan in a single day. Sail Rock and the oil platform are the favourites. More marine life, less time travelling.

Ten years, six languages

The instructors

International
experience,
local knowledge

The centre opened in 2024, but the team behind it has more than a decade underwater. We have worked in some of the world's best dive destinations and we apply the same standard here: clear briefings, dive sites chosen by the tide, and realistic plans rather than textbook ones.

Between us we speak Spanish, English, French, Dutch, Hebrew and Russian, with backgrounds in Egypt, the Philippines, Cambodia, Vietnam and Indonesia. That global perspective is mostly useful for one thing: explaining marine life, currents and technique in a way that makes sense to someone who has never worn a mask.

  • From Try Dive to Advanced SSI courses
  • Maximum four students per instructor
  • We tailor each outing to your level
  • Photography, deep, or a slow dive for turtles — your call

Safety and equipment

New gear,
checked daily

As a 2024 centre, all of our equipment is new — and it is checked every single day. Regulators, BCDs and wetsuits come in XS to XXL, with official SSI servicing from day one. Turn up with a swimsuit; we have the rest in your size.
